AZET Pass Lite is a free, open-source password manager: it stores your logins, cards and notes end to end encrypted, fills them in apps and browsers, and shows two-factor codes, with every feature included.

HOW IT DIFFERS
• From the browser's built-in password list: your vault works in every app and browser on the phone, and on your other devices through pass.azet.io.
• From managers that keep two-factor codes for paying users: authenticator (TOTP) codes are included for everyone.
• Open source: built on the Bitwarden code (GPL-3.0); the source is on github.com/neulketing/azet-pass.

WHAT IT DOES
• Unlimited logins, cards, identities, secure notes and SSH keys
• Autofill in apps and browsers through Android's autofill service
• Built-in authenticator: two-factor (TOTP) codes next to each login
• Password, passphrase and username generator
• Send: share text or files with an expiry date
• Passkeys, biometric unlock and an auto-lock timer
• Folders, search and favourites

SECURITY
• Zero-knowledge, end-to-end encryption: your master password never leaves the device, and AZET cannot read your vault
• Account and vault are stored on pass.azet.io (AZET LLC); you can also connect a self-hosted server

AZET Pass Lite is free.
