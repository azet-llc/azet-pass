AZET Pass keeps your passwords, passkeys, verification codes, cards and addresses in one vault and fills them in when you sign in.

Sign in faster
• Fills user names and passwords in apps and in Chrome
• Keeps authenticator (TOTP) codes next to each login and puts the code into the code box
• Saves and uses passkeys (Android 14 or newer)
• Fills your card number and address at checkout in one tap

Easy to move in
• Scan Google Authenticator's export QR and your accounts arrive with live codes
• Import a .csv file from another app
• Hold a contactless card to the back of the phone to read its number (NFC)

Kept safe
• Your vault is encrypted on the phone before it syncs to pass.azet.io. Your account password never reaches the server, and AZET cannot open your vault
• Unlock with fingerprint or face, auto-lock, screenshot blocking
• Check: finds weak passwords, reused passwords and passwords over a year old
• Password generator
• Send: pass text along through a link that expires
• Export to a file locked with a password

The same vault everywhere
Use the same account on iPhone, iPad, Mac, the browser extension and the web (pass.azet.io).

Free, with no ads. The source is open (GPL-3.0): github.com/azet-llc/azet-pass
